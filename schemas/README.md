# Chattree Official Schemas

This directory is reserved for official JSON Schemas, TypeScript type contracts, and specification definitions governing the Chattree ecosystem.

---

## Planned Schemas

Future schemas hosted here will define and validate:
- **Plugin Manifests**: Structure, metadata, declared capabilities, and entry point schemas.
- **Package Specifications**: Archive packaging requirements, asset structures, and distribution integrity definitions.
- **Configuration & State**: Workspace, tool, and conversational configuration contracts.

---

> **Note:** Official schemas will be published here alongside their respective versioned specifications.
